FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to build my website?+
Most projects are completed within 7–14 business days depending on your package. We'll give you a firm timeline before we start, and we stick to it.
Do I need to provide content and images?+
It helps, but it's not essential. We can write your website copy and source professional imagery as part of the project. Just let us know your preferences during the initial consultation.
Will my website rank on Google?+
Every site includes foundational SEO — on-page optimisation, schema markup, technical SEO, and fast load speeds. For businesses wanting to dominate local search, we recommend pairing your new site with our ongoing SEO management service.
Can I update the website myself?+
Absolutely. We build on platforms that are easy to manage, and we provide full training so you're never dependent on us for day-to-day changes.
Is there ongoing support after launch?+
Every package includes free post-launch support (30–60 days depending on your package). After that, optional maintenance plans start from just £49/month. We're always here when you need us.
